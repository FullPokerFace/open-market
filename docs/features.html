Open Market features.

Open Market is a free, open-source earnings calendar whose features are created and maintained by its community. The calendar runs on live Finnhub data in day, week and month views that fit the screen, with each company marked by when it reports (before the open, during the day, after the close), a Most popular (Fortune 100) and exchange filter, a details panel, a "+N" companies modal, search across this month and the next two, a saved watchlist, your own ticker lists (remembered between visits), and a "Suggest a feature" board where visitors post ideas and vote on them. The header, filters, market panel and day view now work on phones. Everything fetched from Finnhub, SEC, CNN and Kalshi is now saved in MongoDB, so it survives deploys and serverless hosting, and page views are counted with Vercel Analytics. Feature suggestions are now a public board at the top of the Roadmap: OpenAI moderates each idea, writes its card and merges repeats into the idea they match, and visitors vote ideas up or down so the most wanted rise to the top. A Playwright end-to-end suite covers the main flows and runs against localhost or the live site without writing any data. Next up: a market-cap filter, then shareable company links and the week and month views on phones.

MongoDB storageReplaces Next's in-memory "use cache". Everything fetched from Finnhub, SEC, CNN and Kalshi is saved in the earnings-report database on MongoDB Atlas, one collection per kind of data, each document { data, fetchedAt }: earningsCalendar (one per Monday–Sunday week, so months that share a week share it; 1 hour), epsHistory (per symbol; 1 hour), companyProfiles (per symbol, saved when a company is first opened; 1 day), fearGreed and keyReleases (one latest document each; 5 minutes). A saved copy younger than that is used as is; an older one is refetched and overwritten. Requests that arrive together share one fetch, and if a source fails the older saved copy is shown instead of an error. Survives deploys, restarts and serverless hosting; with no visitors, nothing is fetched.stored() in src/lib/db.js
Company directoryOne document per exchange-listed company in companies: { _id: "AAPL", exchange: "XNAS", name, cik, listedAt }, about 13,000. Merges Finnhub's symbol list (which exchange; OTC and closed-end funds left out) with SEC's names and IDs, refreshed together once a day; symbols no longer listed are removed. Pages look up only the companies on their calendar (~1,700 symbols, 70–200ms) instead of loading the whole list. Known gap: while the collection is first filled on an empty database, pages see fewer companies for a few seconds.getCompanyDirectory() in src/lib/companies.js
Market historyEvery fresh Fear & Greed and key-releases fetch is also added to fearGreedHistory and keyReleasesHistory ({ data, fetchedAt }), at most one entry per 5 minutes and only when someone visits, so there are gaps in quiet hours. Kept for charting later; nothing reads them yet, and old entries are never deleted. Add a collection to KEEP_HISTORY to keep history for it too.src/lib/db.js
Warm-upAfter each page is sent, this month and the next two are loaded and saved in the background, so paging ahead and search never wait on Finnhub. Months saved within the hour cost one lookup; a failure only means that month loads when opened.after() in src/app/page.js
Error handlingShows "unavailable" instead of crashing if Finnhub fails and nothing is saved; shows the older saved copy when one exists; falls back to bare tickers and no exchange filter if the company directory fails.src/app/page.js, src/lib/db.js
