Gamma exposure.

GEX is per company, but only for companies with listed options: roughly the 4–5k optionable US names out of the ~13k on the calendar, plus indexes and ETFs (SPX, SPY, QQQ), where most people actually watch it. No free service offers per-ticker GEX at our scale, so we compute it ourselves. Cboe publishes a free, 15-minute-delayed options chain for each ticker with gamma and open interest already filled in. A server function turns that into net GEX, GEX by strike, the call wall, the put wall and the flip level. The result is shown in the details panel next to the earnings data. SPX GEX can go in the market panel. The one blocker before launch is Cboe's terms for showing that data on a public site.

Why it fits an earnings site

  • Shows where dealers may pin or chase price into the report
  • Can be filtered to the first expiry after the report date
  • Sits next to EPS and revenue in the details panel
  • SPX GEX adds market-wide context to Fear & Greed

What "plugin" can't mean

No free embeddable GEX widget turned up, and TradingView has none built in. FlashAlpha's free API (5 requests/day, no SPY or QQQ) is too small for a public page. An "integrated plugin" in practice means our own section, fed by our own calculation.

03 / MathWhat gets computed

How GEX is computed.

This is the standard "naive" model: dealers are assumed to be long the calls and short the puts that customers trade. That's a guess about who holds what, not observed positioning, and the UI should say so.

per contract     gex = sign × gamma × openInterest × 100 × spot² × 0.01     sign: call +1, put −1
                 → dollars of stock dealers would buy (+) or sell (−) for a 1% move

net GEX          Σ gex over all contracts           positive: dealers damp moves · negative: dealers add to moves
by strike        Σ gex grouped by strike            the bar chart
call wall        strike with the largest positive GEX
put wall         strike with the most negative GEX
flip level       strike where cumulative GEX (low → high) crosses from negative to positive
                 v1 approximation; the exact flip re-prices gamma at each hypothetical spot (Black-Scholes), later

AAPL, Oct 27 2026, spot 345.82   net +$0.91B · largest +GEX: 350 ($321M), 360 ($176M), 355 ($136M) · flip ≈ 350

Open interest is set once a day, from the prior session, while gamma and spot change through the day. So intraday GEX moves with price, not with new positions. Contracts are parsed from the OCC symbol in option (for example AAPL261028C00250000: root, YYMMDD expiry, C/P, strike × 1000).
