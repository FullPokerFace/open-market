Where to get earnings data.

Recommendation: start with Finnhub's free plan. One call returns every company reporting in a date range, with EPS and revenue estimate and actual, which is enough for Beat / Mixed / Miss. Move to Financial Modeling Prep Starter (~$19–29/mo) if the free plan's limits or license don't fit.

02 / Why FinnhubPlan for this app
  • One request covers the month. /calendar/earnings?from=2026-10-01&to=2026-10-31 returns date, ticker, before-open / after-close timing, and EPS + revenue estimate and actual.
  • It fits the free plan. Fetch on the server and cache for a few hours: a handful of calls a day, far under 60/min.
  • Company names are missing. Fill them from SEC's free company_tickers.json, one file mapping every US ticker to its name.
  • The chart gets 4 bars, not 8. The free plan returns 4 quarters of EPS surprises; 8 needs a paid plan.
03 / Before going liveChecks
  1. License. Free plans are usually for personal or non-commercial use. Showing their data on a public website often needs a paid plan, so check the terms of whichever service you pick.
  2. Adjusted vs GAAP. Most services compare adjusted EPS against consensus, as DESIGN.md asks. Don't mix in GAAP actuals from SEC filings.
  3. Unofficial feeds. Nasdaq's website feed, and scraper services that resell it, are free but undocumented, can break without warning, and aren't licensed for reuse. Prototype only.
